Risk controls

Performance: Three.js loads only on the homepage, caps pixel ratio, shares geometry, and pauses when hidden.
Cover reliability: textures are cached locally; unresolved books receive deterministic cloth covers rather than broken images.
Accessibility: the complete HTML shelf exists before WebGL and remains the usable fallback on mobile, reduced motion, or renderer failure.
Execution is authorized. Lower-cost agents handle the independent tracks concurrently; the primary agent integrates, runs automated checks, and visually reviews desktop and mobile.
