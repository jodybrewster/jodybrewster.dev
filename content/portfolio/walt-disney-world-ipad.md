An iPad experience for planning a vacation at Walt Disney World Resort, made in 2010, the year the iPad launched.
Everything happens inside an ornate gold frame.
Magic Kingdom, Epcot, Disney's Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom, both water parks and the resort hotels each open in the oval at its center, with photos, video, 360-degree views and fact cards like the one on the Tree of Life.
Side panels lead to prices, special offers, a free planning DVD and DisneyWorld.com.

I was the technical lead.
