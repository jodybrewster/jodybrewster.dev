# Third-party media

The library and other pages show book covers, album art and game art that belong to their publishers, labels and studios, under `public/media/` or fetched from services such as Spotify.

They are used only to identify the works Jody has read, listened to or played, next to his own notes on them, at thumbnail size and without any suggestion of endorsement.
That is the usual basis for showing cover art (identification and commentary, often argued as fair use or nominative use), and it depends on staying that way: no covers used as decoration, for sale or for marketing.

The site's `LICENSE` claims none of this material.
A rights holder who asks for an item to be removed gets it removed, through the contact on https://jodybrewster.dev.
